Precision Energy Engineering

Pioneering Reliable Lithium Power for India

Cell Care India was founded with a singular mission: to eliminate thermal instability, short life cycles, and imported dependence in India's electric mobility and renewable energy sectors.

Operating from our advanced assembly plant in New Delhi, our team merges Tier-1 Grade-A cell chemistries (LiFePO4 & NMC) with programmable Smart BMS technology. Every battery pack undergoes automated capacity grouping, resistance matching, and automated load-bank endurance validation before entering the supply chain.

Our 5-Stage Assembly Standard

How every Cell Care India battery pack is built, welded, tested, and certified.

01

Cell Capacity & IR Matching

Automated cell sorting machine matches internal resistance (mΩ) and milliampere-hour capacity to within 0.5% tolerance.

02

Precision Spot & Laser Welding

Pure copper/nickel busbars welded with pneumatic dual-pulse machinery ensuring virtually zero contact resistance and maximum vibration resilience.

03

Smart BMS Integration

Calibrating overcharge, over-discharge, short circuit, and active balance channels with dual NTC temperature probes.

04

Full Load Bank Cycle Testing

Every pack undergoes a 100% computerized charge-discharge cycle curve test to measure real usable Watt-hours before boxing.

05

IP67 Enclosure & QC Pass

Sealed in heavy-duty powder-coated metal housing with shock absorption EVA foam, laser-etched serial numbering, and warranty tamper tags.
