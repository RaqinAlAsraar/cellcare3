Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Overview

Cell Care India respects your privacy. This privacy policy describes our collection, use, and disclosure of personal and commercial inquiry information when you use our website at cellcareindia.com.

2. Information We Collect

When you request a quotation or contact our team via WhatsApp, we collect information you voluntarily provide, such as your full name, company name, phone number, email address, delivery address, and technical battery requirements.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use this information exclusively to prepare accurate commercial quotations, calculate insured logistics freight, fulfill battery warranty commitments, and communicate with you regarding your order status.

4. WhatsApp Communication

When you click WhatsApp inquiry buttons on our site, you are redirected to the WhatsApp messaging platform operated by Meta. Your chat interactions are subject to WhatsApp's terms of service and end-to-end encryption.

5. Data Protection

We do not sell, rent, or trade customer commercial data to third-party marketing firms. All communications are kept strictly confidential within our engineering and sales administration.

For inquiries regarding privacy or your data, contact us at: sales@cellcareindia.com or call +91 85059 10581.
