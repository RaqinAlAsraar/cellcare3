ENGINEERING // LIFECYCLE

End-to-End Battery Engineering Services

From custom OEM prototyping and BMS firmware calibration to fleet battery diagnostics and green recycling, Cell Care India supports your power needs across every lifecycle stage.

Custom Pack Sizing & Prototyping

Tailored design of Lithium-ion and LiFePO4 packs matching specific voltage profiles, continuous discharge current (C-rates), custom CAD enclosures, and vibration-damped mountings.

Smart BMS Calibration & Programming

Configuration of programmable BMS controllers with CAN-bus, RS485, and Bluetooth telemetry protocols. Calibration of precise over-voltage, thermal cut-off, and active cell balancing.

Diagnostic Health Check & Testing

Full computerized charge-discharge cycle curve analysis, Internal Resistance (IR) impedance measurement, thermal imaging, and capacity degradation grading for commercial fleets.

Fleet Electrification & Conversion

End-to-end retrofitting solutions for commercial fleets, e-rickshaws, and golf carts transitioning from heavy lead-acid batteries to high-efficiency, zero-maintenance lithium packs.

Solar & ESS Storage Integration

Commissioning and integration of wall-mount and rack-mount lithium storage banks with residential and industrial hybrid solar inverters, ensuring seamless backup power.

Eco-Friendly Recycling & Disposal

Government-compliant lithium-ion battery collection and green recycling protocol. We safely recover raw active materials (lithium, iron, copper) through verified environmental processes.

Need Specialized Engineering Assistance?

Our chief battery engineers can evaluate your electrical schematics and provide an engineering report within 24 hours.
